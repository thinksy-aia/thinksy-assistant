Thinksy Personal Assistant uses Hermes Agent to help its owner work with Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive.

Purpose and access

This is a private, personal-use integration for its owner, not a public sign-up service. Google OAuth grants the local assistant access to the services explicitly approved by the owner.

  • Gmail: search and read messages, draft responses, and send or manage messages when instructed.
  • Calendar: read schedules and create, update, or delete events when instructed.
  • Drive: find and read files, and upload or manage files when instructed.

How it works

Hermes runs on the owner's machine. Information retrieved to answer a request may be included in prompts sent to the configured AI provider. Sending messages or changing Google content requires the owner's approval.

This public website contains only information about the integration. It does not sign users in, request Google permissions, or display private Google data.
