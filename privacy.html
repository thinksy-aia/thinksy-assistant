Privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-10-25

This policy covers Thinksy Personal Assistant, a personal-use Google Workspace integration operated by the owner of thinksy.aia@gmail.com, using Hermes Agent.

Google data accessed

With the owner's Google OAuth consent, the assistant can access Gmail message content and metadata, Calendar event details, and Drive file metadata and contents. Access is used to perform owner-requested tasks. The configured permissions also allow sending or managing email, changing calendar events, and managing Drive files, subject to the owner's instructions and approval.

Purpose of processing

Google data is used to answer requests, summarize information, prepare drafts, organize schedules and files, and carry out explicitly approved actions. It is not used for advertising, sold, or used by the operator to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

AI processing and service providers

Relevant Google data may be sent to the configured AI provider as part of prompts and tool results necessary for a request. The current configuration uses Azure AI Foundry, a Microsoft service. Provider-side storage, retention, security, and processing are governed by that service's applicable terms and the owner's account configuration. This policy does not promise zero provider retention. Changes to the configured provider require this policy to be reviewed.

Google processes API requests to its own services. GitHub hosts this informational website and may collect technical request information under its own privacy policy. The website does not transmit Google account data to GitHub.

Storage and security

OAuth client credentials and refresh tokens are stored outside public repositories in the owner's private Hermes profile with owner-only filesystem permissions. Task-relevant Google data may remain in local conversation history, logs, or files the owner asks the assistant to create. This website contains no Google credentials, tokens, messages, calendar events, or private Drive files.

Retention and deletion

Local task history and requested outputs are retained until the owner deletes them; there is no automatic fixed deletion period promised by this integration. The owner can delete local records and outputs, remove stored credentials, or request help through the contact address below. Deletion of local records does not itself delete Google originals or data retained by a service provider under its own terms.

Control and revocation

The owner can revoke OAuth access at any time through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete previously saved local data. Google content is modified only when instructed and approved.

Google API Limited Use

Thinksy Personal Assistant's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Website and contact

This site uses no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, cookies, or sign-in forms added by the operator. GitHub's hosting infrastructure may log requests. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions or deletion requests: thinksy.aia@gmail.com.
